Refund Policy

Last Updated: August 09, 2026

1. Overview

At U.S. Career Academy, we are committed to delivering high-quality online training programs in Business Analysis, Data Analytics, Excel, Power BI, Python, SQL, Tableau, AI Tools, Communication Skills, and related career development courses.

Please read this Refund Policy carefully before enrolling in any course or making any payment.

2. Course Enrollment and Payments

Enrollment is confirmed only after successful payment of the applicable course fees. By making a payment, you acknowledge that you have reviewed the course details, fee structure, and applicable policies.

3. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be considered under the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate payment made by mistake.
  • Technical issues resulting in duplicate course enrollment.
  • Course cancellation initiated by U.S. Career Academy.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will generally not be provided in the following cases:

  • After access has been granted to course materials or recordings.
  • After attending one or more live training sessions.
  • Failure to attend scheduled classes.
  • Change of mind after enrollment.
  • Lack of interest in the course.
  • Personal scheduling conflicts.
  • Failure to secure employment despite placement assistance.
  • Dissatisfaction due to unmet personal expectations not explicitly promised by U.S. Career Academy.

5. Course Cancellation by U.S. Career Academy

If a course is cancelled by U.S. Career Academy and no alternative batch or replacement program is offered, eligible students may receive a full refund of the amount paid.

6. Placement Assistance Disclaimer

U.S. Career Academy provides 100% Placement Assistance through resume reviews, career guidance, interview preparation, job referrals, and professional development support.

Placement assistance does not guarantee employment, interview calls, salary packages, or job offers. Therefore, refunds will not be issued based on employment outcomes.

7. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed using the original payment method whenever possible.

  • Refund processing may take 7 to 15 business days.
  • Payment gateway charges, banking charges, or applicable taxes may be deducted where applicable.
  • The credited amount may vary depending on payment provider policies.

8. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us with:

  • Full Name
  • Registered Email Address
  • Registered Mobile Number
  • Course Name
  • Payment Receipt or Transaction ID
  • Reason for Refund Request

9. Policy Changes

U.S. Career Academy reserves the right to modify this Refund Policy at any time without prior notice. Updated versions will be published on this page.
